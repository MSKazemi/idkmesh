## What maintainership means

Maintainers are responsible for more than merging code. They are expected to:

- protect technical quality and security;
- help contributors succeed;
- review in a timely and respectful way;
- make decisions publicly and document major reasoning;
- reduce dependency on themselves by developing reviewers and future maintainers;
- value research, documentation, security, testing, design, and community work alongside code;
- consider community impact in major changes.

## Becoming a maintainer

See [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md). There is no fixed commit-count threshold. Sustained responsibility, judgment, collaboration, review quality, and community stewardship matter more than raw contribution volume.

## Future structure

As activity grows, this file should list scoped maintainers for subprojects/areas and Community Stewards. It should also record emeritus maintainers so historical contribution remains visible without leaving inactive people as required approvers.
