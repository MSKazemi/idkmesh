## Interpretation guardrails

- `loses badly` is a diagnostic rule, not a significance test.
- The 100,000-worker cells are protocol/scalability probes with capped arrival rates, not full-capacity saturation experiments.
- `fresh`, `moderate`, and `stale` change several stressors together; use factor-isolated experiments for causal claims.
- A single seed is useful for a reproducible reference but insufficient for strong generalization.
