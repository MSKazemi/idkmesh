# IDKMesh Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

IDKMesh is committed to a welcoming, collaborative, and intellectually honest community for people with different backgrounds, experience levels, disciplines, languages, and viewpoints.

We value disagreement about ideas. We do not accept harassment, intimidation, personal attacks, discrimination, or behavior that makes participation unsafe.

## Expected behavior

Community members should:

- be respectful and constructive;
- criticize ideas, evidence, code, and processes rather than people;
- make room for newcomers and people who communicate differently;
- state uncertainty rather than pretending confidence;
- give credit for ideas and contributions;
- avoid flooding discussions or reviews with low-quality generated content;
- respect privacy and do not publish private information without permission;
- accept reasonable moderator or maintainer requests intended to keep collaboration productive.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples include:

- harassment, threats, stalking, or intimidation;
- discriminatory or demeaning comments;
- sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention;
- deliberate public disclosure of another person's private information;
- sustained personal attacks or bad-faith disruption;
- impersonation, deceptive attribution, or deliberate manipulation of contribution/reputation systems;
- using humans or AI agents to spam, brigade, or overwhelm project communication and review systems.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when a person is officially representing IDKMesh in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers and designated Community Stewards are responsible for clarifying standards and may remove content, restrict participation, lock conversations, or take other proportionate actions when necessary.

Enforcement decisions should consider safety, severity, repeated behavior, context, and the possibility of repair. Sensitive reports should not be posted as public issues.

Until IDKMesh establishes a dedicated private community-reporting channel, use GitHub's built-in reporting/blocking mechanisms and contact a listed maintainer privately when possible. The project should add a dedicated confidential reporting route as the maintainer team grows.

## Good-faith disagreement

Strong technical disagreement is not a conduct violation by itself. IDKMesh should preserve space for dissent, negative results, and criticism when expressed constructively and without harassment.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by common open-source community codes of conduct and is intentionally concise for the project's bootstrap phase. It should evolve with the community and its moderation capacity.
