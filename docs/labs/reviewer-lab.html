What this lab demonstrates — and what it does not

A real finding about these invented votes: even unanimous-looking agreement can be mistaken when reviewers miss the same item. A stricter voting rule changes the mix of false approvals and false rejections. But it is not the same thing as independent verification. Shared mistakes here are simple overlaps, not a statistical estimate of reviewer independence or causation.

The real IDKMesh tool: idkmesh gate-audit analyzes a ground-truthed verdict matrix and also reports correlation, effective votes and limitations. This browser lab deliberately does not reproduce those advanced statistics or claim to verify code. Neither interface runs reviewers, decides correctness for unknown work, grants approval authority, or merges anything.

The editable selection here changes the count of recorded votes only. The fixture and known correct outcomes stay unchanged. To reproduce or modify the actual input, see the source JSON and Getting Started.
