# Operationalizing repository collaboration observables

Date: 2026-08-29

## Project-owner request

Work professionally across unclaimed repository issues, merge verified changes,
and close only completed issues.

## Selected issue and interpretation

Issue 86 asks that mathematical concepts become replaceable, measurable
repository mechanisms. The selected bounded gap was the missing first-class
review latency, concentration, recurrence, ownership, queue, CI, and structural
debt layer, plus preregistration of the proposed latency-to-recurrence study.

## Decisions

- Preserve normalized observations and explicit evidence mass rather than emit
  unexplained confidence numbers.
- Use deterministic bootstrap intervals only for observed medians, Beta-binomial
  summaries for bounded rates, and label HHI/queue/debt as descriptive state.
- Derive provisional strategy weights from verified outcomes, never activity.
- Preregister the community association study before outcome collection and
  explicitly deny a causal interpretation.

## Remaining program gates

Issue 86 remains open: production policy still uses configured priors, real
heterogeneous worker/verifier correlation evidence is absent, and the
preregistered study has no enrolled outcome cohort yet.

## Community impact

The fixture and standard-library CLI make the measurement definitions auditable
without credentials or paid compute. Privacy minimization and no-authority
outputs reduce incentives to turn volunteer activity into a ranking system.
